QR Code & Barcode Scanner

Scan a QR code or a barcode from an image, in your browser. The image is decoded on this device by a WebAssembly module: it is never uploaded. Decoded links are shown as text and never opened.

Choose an image

You can also drop an image file on this box.

full runs every stage and scores how well a QR code scans; fast skips the deep QR stages; frame only decodes, as for camera frames. Every scan here runs to its end, without a time budget.
