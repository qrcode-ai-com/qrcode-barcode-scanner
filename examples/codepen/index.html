QR Code & Barcode Scanner

Scan the QR codes and barcodes in an image, in your browser. The open-source scanner engine is a WebAssembly module loaded from jsDelivr at an exact version; your image is decoded on this device and never uploaded. A decoded link is shown as text and never opened.
